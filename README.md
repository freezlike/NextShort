Tired of manually scrolling to the next YouTube Short? NextShort is a lightweight, premium extension that puts your viewing experience on autopilot. 

Whether you are cooking, working out, multitasking, or just relaxing, NextShort ensures the entertainment never stops. It automatically detects when a Short finishes playing and instantly swipes to the next one. No more repetitive clicking or scrolling!

🚀 KEY FEATURES:
• Smart Auto-Skip: Instantly moves to the next video the exact moment the current one ends.
• Anti-Freeze & Anti-Buffering: Detects if a video is stuck loading, broken, or unavailable, and automatically skips it after 5 seconds to keep your feed moving smoothly.
• Anti-AFK Built-In: Automatically dismisses the annoying "Video paused. Continue watching?" prompt.
• Background Tab Support: Powered by an optimized background Web Worker, ensuring flawless auto-scrolling even when you are browsing on another tab or minimizing the window.
• Zero Configuration: Just "set it and forget it". Install the extension, open YouTube Shorts, and let it do the magic.

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST:
NextShort respects your digital privacy. The code is completely open-source and runs strictly locally on your browser.
